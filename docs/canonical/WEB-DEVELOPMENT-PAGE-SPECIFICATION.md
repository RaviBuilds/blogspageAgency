## Canonical Master Specification

**Document status:** Final strategic specification --- implementation
source of truth\
**Version:** 1.0\
**Target page:** `/services/web-development`\
**Brand:** BLOGSPAGE AI\
**Primary audience:** Business owners, founders, operators, and
decision-makers who need a website or web system that performs business
functions\

global clients\
**Implementation intent:** Build once from this specification; do not
redesign the information architecture during implementation unless a
concrete technical contradiction is discovered.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Purpose of This Document

This document is the single source of truth for the BLOGSPAGE AI Web
Development service page.

The page must explain **web development as the working layer of a
digital business**, not as a list of programming languages.

The visitor should understand:

-   what can be built,
-   what level of system they actually need,
-   what happens behind a website,
-   what business functions can be connected,
-   how customers, staff, admins, payments, data and external services
    can work together,
-   what BLOGSPAGE AI has actually built,
-   what technologies support those systems,
-   how projects are delivered,
-   what ownership and post-launch considerations exist,
-   and how to start a project.

The page must remain understandable to a non-technical business owner
while still demonstrating enough technical depth for a technical
founder, CTO, developer, or advisor to recognize that the work is
genuine.

# 4. Non-Negotiable Page Principles

## 4.1 Outcome before technology

Always explain:

**What the business/user experiences → what happens behind the scenes →
technology tag if useful.**

Example:

> Customers can book online while the team manages reservations from one
> place.

Then, if needed:

> Booking logic, database, payment integration and admin workflows power
> the experience.

Small tags can identify technologies.

Do not begin with:

> Next.js + Node.js + PostgreSQL + Supabase...

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.2 Do not repeat architecture explanations

Avoid separate sections that all explain:

-   frontend,
-   backend,
-   database,
-   API,
-   authentication,
-   integrations.

These belong primarily in one consolidated architecture section.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.3 No technology syllabus

The page is not a tutorial.

Do not explain what HTML, CSS, JavaScript, APIs, databases or JWT are in
textbook detail.

Explain their business purpose.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.4 No exaggerated claims

Avoid:

-   best,
-   #1,
-   top,
-   cutting-edge,
-   revolutionary,
-   enterprise-grade unless demonstrably appropriate,
-   Silicon Valley-level,
-   guaranteed results,
-   guaranteed rankings,
-   inflated project counts,
-   invented statistics.

Use specific capabilities and real systems instead.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.5 No portfolio repetition from Web Design

Do NOT reuse:

-   Neodent dental website,
-   Best100Movies,
-   other Web Design proof examples.

The Web Development page has three primary proof systems:

1.  **ArogyaDiet**
2.  **NextInn**
3.  **Pixel AI**

Do not turn these into generic portfolio cards. They are capability
demonstrations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.6 No WordPress positioning

Do not mention WordPress anywhere on this page.

Do not say:

-   "we don't use WordPress",
-   "instead of WordPress",
-   "WordPress alternatives",
-   "legacy WordPress",
-   or similar.

The page should simply present the technologies and architecture
BLOGSPAGE AI actually uses.

Do not intentionally target WordPress keywords.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.7 Do not over-technicalize hosting

Do not present:

-   shared hosting,
-   dedicated server,
-   VPS

as headline selling points.

Infrastructure can be described as:

-   managed hosting,
-   cloud infrastructure,
-   deployment infrastructure,
-   scalable hosting where required.

Specific infrastructure options can appear in the technology/FAQ layer
if useful.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.8 Do not over-sell AI

AI should appear when it performs a real function.

Examples:

-   AI image generation,
-   AI image restoration,
-   AI-powered product features,
-   AI APIs,
-   AI-assisted workflows.

Do not place an "AI" node in every diagram merely to make the page look
modern.

# 16. Section 09 --- Right-Sizing + FAQ

## Purpose

Show restraint.

A premium development company should be comfortable telling a client
when a custom platform is unnecessary.

Core message:

> Not every business needs a custom application. We first understand the
> problem, then recommend the simplest system that can solve it
> properly.

Potential FAQ topics:

### Do I need a business website or a web application?

Explain based on functionality and workflow.

### Can you add login to an existing website?

Yes, where the existing architecture and requirements support it.

### Can my website accept payments?

Yes, through supported payment providers such as Razorpay or Stripe,
depending on the project.

### Can you build booking or ordering systems?

Yes.

### Can you create separate customer and admin dashboards?

Yes.

### Can different users have different permissions?

Yes, through role-based access and appropriate authentication
architecture.

### Can you build a multi-tenant system?

Yes, where the business model requires one platform to serve multiple
businesses, branches or tenants.

### Can you integrate existing tools?

Yes, when APIs/webhooks or other supported integration mechanisms are
available.

### Can you build an MVP first?

Yes. MVP scope should focus on the smallest useful product that can be
launched and evaluated.

### Who owns the code?

Answer according to the actual engagement/contract terms. Do not make an
unconditional legal promise inside the page unless the business terms
guarantee it.

### Can the system grow later?

Explain modular development and planned evolution.

### Do you work only in Hyderabad?

Position BLOGSPAGE AI as Hyderabad-based and able to work with
businesses beyond Hyderabad where appropriate.
