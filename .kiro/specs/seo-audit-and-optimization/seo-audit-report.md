# Blogspage SEO Audit Report

## Pre-remediation

### The rating: 3.0 out of 10

The site scores **3.0 / 10** against the ten-category rubric published below. That number is low, and it is not a verdict on the quality of the work already done. The blog surface of this site is genuinely well built: per-post metadata with canonicals and robots directives, three kinds of valid structured data, a deep SEO-aware content model in Sanity, and a publishing filter that keeps drafts, scheduled posts, and hidden posts out of the sitemap. Several agencies never get that far.

The score is dragged down by three things that sit upstream of all of it.

1. **Duplicate URL control (1.02 points lost).** The solution page router accepts *any* city token. `/solutions/gym-business-solution-website-at-anything` renders a live page that declares itself canonical. That is an unbounded set of duplicate pages competing with each other. On top of it, four of the site's most important pages — the homepage, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms` — declare no canonical URL at all.
2. **Crawl coverage (0.90 points lost).** The ten `/solutions/...` local-SEO pages, which are the pages built to win commercial searches, are **absent from `sitemap.xml` entirely**. The sitemap lists the homepage, the blog index, the three legal pages, and every post — and nothing else.
3. **The business entity (0.85 points lost).** Nowhere on the site does the markup say "this is an organisation called Blogspage, here is its logo, here are its social profiles, here is its address". There is no `Organization`, no `WebSite`, no `LocalBusiness` structured data. Search engines and AI answer engines are left to infer the agency's identity from prose.

Fixing those three costs less than the work already invested in the blog, and they are what stand between the site and its existing content earning rankings.

## Existing strengths

These are things the site already does correctly. They are listed separately from the Findings so the remediation work does not accidentally undo them.

1. **Root metadata is properly grounded.** `metadataBase`, a title template, keywords, and full Open Graph and Twitter card blocks are declared once at the root — `src/app/layout.tsx`.
2. **Per-post metadata is genuinely thorough.** `generateMetadata` emits canonical, `robots.index`/`robots.follow` driven by the post's own Sanity flags, article-typed Open Graph with `publishedTime` and `modifiedTime`, keywords, and authors — `src/app/(site)/blogs/[slug]/page.tsx`.
3. **Three valid structured-data builders.** `BlogPosting` (with `wordCount`, `timeRequired`, `articleSection`), `BreadcrumbList`, and a conditional `FAQPage` that returns `null` rather than an empty node — `src/lib/blog.ts`.
4. **Solution pages already carry structured data and a canonical.** A `ProfessionalService` node with `areaServed` plus an absolute canonical URL — `src/app/(site)/solutions/[slug]/page.tsx`.
5. **A dynamic sitemap and robots file.** Post routes derive `lastModified` from real content dates and `changeFrequency` from the `evergreen` flag; `/studio` is disallowed — `src/app/sitemap.ts`, `src/app/robots.ts`.
6. **One publishing filter, reused everywhere.** `LIVE_POST_FILTER` centralises "what is publicly visible" — not a draft, has a `publishedAt`, not future-dated — and `POST_SITEMAP_QUERY` adds `noindex != true` on top of it — `src/sanity/lib/queries.ts`.
7. **An SEO-aware content model.** The `post` schema carries excerpt, focus keyword, SEO title and description, canonical override, `noindex`/`nofollow`, `lastReviewed`, FAQ entries, related posts, and required image `alt` text — `src/sanity/schemaTypes/postType.ts`.
8. **Legacy URL equity is preserved.** WordPress root-level post URLs and the singular `/blog` paths are permanently redirected to `/blogs`, with static asset extensions excluded from the catch-all — `next.config.ts`.
9. **A deterministic slug contract for local SEO.** Slug templates are declared once per vertical and resolved through one `slugify` function, so the ten solution routes are reproducible rather than hand-maintained — `src/lib/niches.ts`.
10. **Listing images already reserve their box.** The blog index uses `next/image` with `fill` and an explicit `sizes` string, and falls back to a text tile when a post has no image — `src/app/(site)/blogs/page.tsx`.
11. **Scheduled and hidden posts behave correctly.** A future `publishedAt` keeps a post out of listings and the sitemap; `noindex: true` produces a real `noindex` directive on the post route — `src/sanity/lib/queries.ts`, `src/app/(site)/blogs/[slug]/page.tsx`.

## Findings

Every Finding carries a stable id, exactly one severity, exactly one rubric category, at least one repository file path that exists in the audited commit, and at least one observable consequence for crawling, indexation, ranking, or rendering. Severity follows the rules in Requirement 1.12:

- `critical` — makes an intended indexable route unreachable or unindexable, exposes an unbounded set of duplicate indexable routes, or emits structured data that fails to parse.
- `high` — an indexable route is absent from the crawl layer, resolves to a broken referenced asset, or omits a canonical URL.
- `medium` — a signal is present but incomplete or inconsistent across routes.
- `low` — the defect changes no indexable route's crawling, indexation, or rendering outcome.

### Critical

#### F-01 — The solution router accepts any city token, creating an unbounded duplicate URL space

- **Severity:** `critical` (exposes an unbounded set of duplicate indexable routes)
- **Category:** Canonicalisation and Duplicate Control
- **Paths:** `src/lib/niches.ts`, `src/app/(site)/solutions/[slug]/page.tsx`
- **Observed:** `getNicheBySlug` builds its matcher from each niche's slug template with the city segment captured as `(?<city>[a-z0-9-]+)` and no membership check. `generateStaticParams` pre-renders only the Hyderabad set, but nothing constrains the dynamic set.
- **Consequence:** Any request matching a niche template with an arbitrary trailing token renders HTTP 200 and emits `alternates.canonical` pointing at itself, so each variant self-canonicalises as a distinct page. The result is an unlimited set of near-identical indexable URLs competing for the same query, splitting link and relevance signals and consuming crawl budget that should go to the ten real pages.
- **Live verification:** The size of the already-indexed duplicate set cannot be read from the repository. Observation needed: the Search Console Pages report filtered to `/solutions/`, listing indexed URLs whose city token is not `hyderabad`.
- **Resolves under:** Req 3.3, 3.4, 3.5, 3.6

### High

#### F-02 — `/og-image.png` is referenced by root metadata but absent from the deployment

- **Severity:** `high` (an indexable route resolves to a broken referenced asset)
- **Category:** Metadata and Social Previews
- **Paths:** `src/app/layout.tsx`, `public/blogspage-logo.png` (evidence that `public/` contains no `og-image.png`)
- **Observed:** Root `openGraph.images[0].url` and `twitter.images[0]` both point at `/og-image.png`. The `public/` directory contains `ArogyaDiet.jpg`, `NextInn.jpg`, `blogspage-logo.png`, `favicon.ico`, four gym placeholder SVGs, `movieDB.png`, and `phixlAI.jpg` — and no `og-image.png`.
- **Consequence:** Every route that inherits root metadata — the homepage, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, and every solution page — advertises a social preview image that returns 404. Shares on LinkedIn, X, Slack, and WhatsApp render as a bare text link, which measurably suppresses click-through on exactly the channels the agency uses for outbound.
- **Resolves under:** Req 4.1, 4.2, 4.9

#### F-03 — Four indexable routes declare no canonical URL

- **Severity:** `high` (omits a canonical URL)
- **Category:** Canonicalisation and Duplicate Control
- **Paths:** `src/app/(site)/page.tsx`, `src/app/(site)/contact/page.tsx`, `src/app/(site)/privacy/page.tsx`, `src/app/(site)/terms/page.tsx`
- **Observed:** `/contact`, `/privacy`, and `/terms` export a `metadata` object with `title` and `description` only. The homepage exports no `metadata` at all and inherits the root block, which carries no `alternates`.
- **Consequence:** With no canonical declared, any URL variant that reaches these routes — a tracking query string, an uppercase path, a trailing slash, `www` versus apex — is a candidate for separate indexing, and the engine picks the canonical itself. The homepage is the site's strongest page and the one most likely to be linked with campaign parameters attached.
- **Resolves under:** Req 3.1, 3.2

#### F-04 — Every solution page is missing from `sitemap.xml`

## How to read the score

The rubric scores compliance against a demanding target, not against a typical small-business website. A 10 in any category means every acceptance criterion in the specification is met, including things most sites never do — a connected entity graph, `llms.txt`, a published keyword map, per-route generated social previews. A 3.0 therefore means "the foundations are partly there and the highest-leverage items are unbuilt", not "the site is broken".

The practical reading: the blog engine is the strongest part of this codebase and needs the least work. The commercial pages — the ten solution verticals and the four services the footer already advertises — are where the gap between what the site sells and what search engines can see is widest. Three fixes (bound the slug space, list the solution routes in the sitemap, declare the organisation) move the score more than any content programme would.
